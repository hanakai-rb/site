I'm so happy to share that [**thoughtbot**](https://thoughtbot.com) is our newest silver sponsor!

If you've written Ruby for any length of time, thoughtbot has most likely helped you out. Maybe it was a [blog post](https://thoughtbot.com/blog), an episode of [The Bike Shed](https://bikeshed.thoughtbot.com), one of their many [open source gems](https://thoughtbot.com/open-source), or one of the [lovely humans](https://thoughtbot.com/team) who work there. They've given our community so much for so long.

Now they're backing Hanakai too. Here's Rob Whittaker on why:

> thoughtbot has built web apps and open-source Ruby libraries for over 20 years. We know how much any framework depends on the people who maintain it. Ruby is stronger with a diversity of thoughts, opinions, and approaches. The Hanakai projects show there's more than one good way to build a Ruby app. We're glad to help keep that work going.
>
> — Rob Whittaker, Director of Software Development, [thoughtbot](https://thoughtbot.com)

Thank you, Rob, and thank you to everyone at thoughtbot! It means a lot to have you with us, and your support helps us keep Ruby a place where lots of ideas can grow.

If you or your company would like to join thoughtbot in supporting our work, take a look at our [sponsorship options](/sponsor). There's something for every budget, and we'd love to have you. 🌸
